# IntelliLearn - AI-Powered Learning Management System

## 🎯 What is IntelliLearn?

IntelliLearn is a modern Learning Management System (LMS) that combines traditional course management with artificial intelligence to enhance both teaching and learning experiences. It helps instructors manage courses more efficiently while providing students with personalized support and instant feedback.

---

## 👥 User Roles

### Students
- Access course materials and lessons
- Take assessments and quizzes
- Track grades and progress
- Get AI-powered assistance
- Receive personalized learning recommendations

### Instructors
- Create and manage courses
- Upload lesson materials (PDFs, videos, documents)
- Design assessments with various question types
- Monitor student progress and performance
- View at-risk student predictions

### Administrators
- Manage users and courses
- Oversee system operations
- Access system-wide analytics

---

## 📚 Core Features

### Course Management
- Create courses with lessons and modules
- Upload various file types (PDF, DOCX, videos)
- Organize content with topics and schedules
- Post announcements to students
- Track student enrollment

### Assessment System
- **3 Assessment Types:**
  - Quizzes - Regular knowledge checks
  - Long Exams - Major examinations
  - Individual Activities - Assignments and projects

- **4 Question Types:**
  - Multiple Choice
  - True/False
  - Short Answer
  - Essay (AI-graded)

- **Features:**
  - Automatic grading for objective questions
  - AI-powered essay evaluation
  - Time limits and attempt restrictions
  - Instant feedback and scoring

### Grading & Analytics
- Automatic grade computation
- Weighted scoring: Quiz 40%, Exam 40%, Activity 20%
- Real-time progress tracking
- Performance analytics per course
- Grade reports and transcripts

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Features

### 1. **AI Course Assistant (Chatbot)**
- Answers student questions 24/7
- Context-aware based on lesson materials
- Helps with course content, grades, deadlines
- Natural conversation interface

### 2. **Automatic Essay Grading**
- AI evaluates essay responses
- Uses uploaded lesson materials as reference
- Provides detailed feedback
- Saves instructor time on grading

### 3. **At-Risk Student Prediction**
- Identifies students who may need help
- Based on quiz scores, activity, and engagement
- Helps instructors intervene early
- Provides specific risk factors

### 4. **Personalized Learning Recommendations**
- Suggests study materials for struggling students
- Topic-specific resources
- Adaptive to performance levels

### 5. **Instant Quiz Feedback**
- Analyzes performance after submission
- Identifies weak topics
- Provides study suggestions
- Encourages improvement
